Why I built Plottie

Hi, I’m Juanma. I built Plottie because I saw a lot of room for improvement in project management tools. Too many one-to-ones, and too little visibility into what everyone else is doing, especially as companies grow.

Then I tried to buy a project management tool, and per-seat pricing made it a mess to manage across all the teams I’m part of.

So I made this. One price, and that’s it. Your AI connects to it (through MCP), so you can look at any project, update it, tag people, comment, and add new ones. Much more freedom.

Frequently asked.

How is this different from Asana or Monday? ↓
Asana and Monday started as task trackers. I built Plottie to start from the roadmap and work down to the task, so leaders can see the plan without a status meeting.
Do you charge per AI query? ↓
No. You connect the AI you already use (ChatGPT, Claude, and so on) through MCP. It can read and update work, tag people, comment, and add projects. I don't sell metered queries.
What do you mean by hierarchy? ↓
Plottie mirrors how your company is organised: company, departments, teams, and projects. Each level has its own roadmap and rolls up into the one above. A leader sees the whole picture. A team lead sees their own area.
Does the AI see my roadmap data? ↓
Only through the AI you connect. Plottie has no built-in model. You bring your own (ChatGPT, Claude, and so on). That AI can read and update work, tag people, comment, and add projects, and that provider's data terms apply.
Can I migrate from an existing tool? ↓
Yes. You can import tasks and projects from CSV. If you're on an older single-tenant Plottie instance, there's a migration script that brings your projects, tasks and notes across, history included.
What happens to my data if I cancel? ↓
When your trial ends or you cancel, your workspace goes read-only. Nothing is deleted, and you have 30 days to export everything as CSV.
Do you support invoice billing for procurement? ↓
Yes, on the Enterprise plan. Invoices are in euros, with a DPA included. If procurement needs a security questionnaire, get in touch before signing.
