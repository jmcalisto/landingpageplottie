Frequently asked.

How is this different from Asana or Monday? ↓
Plottie is built for one job: keeping leadership and teams aligned on the plan without status meetings. Asana and Monday started as task trackers and grew outward from there — Plottie started from the roadmap and works down to the task. Different starting points, different shape of product. The tools are designed to coexist, and Plottie's data and hosting live in the EU (Hetzner, Nuremberg), which matters for regulated-industry buyers who can't put roadmap data on US-owned platforms.
Do you charge per AI query? ↓
No. AI in Plottie is bring-your-own through a connector (ChatGPT, Claude, and so on). We don't sell metered built-in queries.
What do you mean by organisational hierarchy? ↓
Plottie mirrors how real organisations work — Organisation → Division → Department → Team → Programme → Project. Each level has its own roadmap and rolls up into the one above. A CLO can see the entire L&D operation in one view. A team lead sees only their area. Connect your own AI and you can ask across the whole hierarchy — "what's the status of the digital transformation programme across all regions?" — and get an answer that aggregates automatically. No spreadsheet, no status meeting.
Does the AI see my roadmap data? ↓
Only through the AI you connect. Plottie has no built-in model: you bring your own through a connector (ChatGPT, Claude, and so on), and that provider's own data terms apply.
Can I migrate from an existing tool? ↓
Yes. Plottie supports CSV import for tasks and projects. If you're on an older single-tenant Plottie instance, we provide a migration script that moves your projects, tasks, and notes into the new platform with historical audit trail preserved.
Do you support invoice billing for procurement? ↓
Yes, on the Enterprise plan. We issue quarterly or annual invoices in EUR with a DPA included as standard. If your procurement process requires a vendor security questionnaire, get in touch before signing.
What happens to my data if I cancel? ↓
Your data is never deleted on downgrade — the product goes read-only so you can still see your roadmap. On cancellation, you have 30 days to export everything via CSV before removal. We don't hold data hostage.
